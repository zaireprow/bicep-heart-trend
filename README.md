## What the trend is

The bicep heart took off on TikTok in late October 2026. You smile at the camera, flex one arm, and curl your other hand against the bicep so that the two shapes form a heart. Most original clips use an original sound from **@trendformusic**.

## Pose: step by step

1. Frame yourself **waist up** with **both arms visible**.
2. Raise one arm into a bicep flex.
3. Curl the fingers of your other hand and press them against the flexed bicep.
4. Adjust until the finger curve and the muscle curve read as a heart.
5. Hold the pose for a beat and keep a straight, sincere smile.

## Tips for a better clip

- **Shoot vertical (9:16).** It's built for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
- **Light the arms.** Shadows across the bicep hide the heart shape.
- **Keep it short.** A few seconds is enough; the payoff is the hold.
- **Sound goes on last.** Add the trend audio in the app when you post.
- **One subject.** The format reads best with a single person or pet in frame.

## FAQ

**Do I need big arms?**
No. The heart comes from the hand shape. If you want the joke version, a generator can exaggerate the arms.

**Can my dog or cat do it?**
With a generator, yes. Cats and dogs work best. The paw makes a rough heart, which is part of the humor.

**Can I put two people, or me and my pet, in one video?**
Not with Bicep Hearts: each video has one subject only.

**Why is the generated video silent?**
So you can add the trending sound yourself on the platform where you post.

**Whose photos can I use?**
